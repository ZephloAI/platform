# Zephlo for SaaS Onboarding — Landing Page Prompt

## Context
This is a vertical-specific landing page for Zephlo targeting SaaS companies who want to improve their user onboarding. The page speaks directly to SaaS founders and product teams struggling with activation rates and onboarding dropoff.

**URL:** zephlo.ai/saas-onboarding (or zephlo.ai/solutions/saas-onboarding)

**Target audience:** SaaS founders, product managers, growth teams, heads of product at B2B SaaS companies.

**Tone:** Empathetic to the pain, confident in the solution. More specific and tactical than the main landing page.
