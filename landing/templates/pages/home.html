01 — WHAT AN AGENT DOES

Answering is the floor. Acting is the product.

L1

Answers

Grounded in a vector index of your actual site. Every answer cites its source page — no hallucinated URLs, no stale docs.

L2

Navigates

Takes the visitor to the right page instead of pasting a link. Deep-links into your app, scrolls to the setting, opens the panel.

L3

Executes

Fills forms, changes settings, files requests, completes multi-step workflows — via tools you register, running client-side in the visitor’s session.

02 — HOW IT WORKS

Live in minutes, not months.

  1. 01

    Point us at your URL

    Our crawler discovers every page, chunks it, and embeds it into a per-Agent vector index. Re-crawl on a schedule or on demand.

  2. 02

    Drop in the widget

    One script tag and your Agent is live. Use the inline element for embedded surfaces — any framework. 

    <script src="https://unpkg.com/zephlo" data-agent-id="YOUR_AGENT_UUID"></script>
  3. 03

    Register tools

    Declare browser-side tools with the SDK — fill forms, navigate, mutate state. The agent composes them to finish real tasks. WebMCP-ready.
